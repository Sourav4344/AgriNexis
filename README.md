# 🌾 AgriNexis

### Not Just the Best Price. The Best Decision.

AgriNexis is an intelligent **market-linkage, price-discovery and transaction-enablement platform** designed for farmers, FPOs and agricultural buyers.

The platform goes beyond simply showing the highest market price.

AgriNexis evaluates market prices, buyer demand, price trends, transportation costs, storage costs and other transaction expenses to recommend the option that gives the farmer the **highest actual earning**.

# 🎯 The Problem

Farmers often face several challenges while selling agricultural produce:

- Limited visibility of market prices
- Difficulty identifying reliable buyers
- Dependency on intermediaries
- Lack of transparent price discovery
- High transportation costs
- Limited access to storage facilities
- Difficulty deciding when and where to sell
- Price fluctuations
- Lack of integrated transaction support

Simply knowing the highest quoted market price does not guarantee the highest farmer income.

For example, a distant buyer may offer a better price but high transportation costs can reduce the farmer's actual earnings.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

AgriNexis helps farmers answer one important question:

> **Where, when and to whom should I sell my crop to get the best actual return?**

The platform analyzes:

- Current market prices
- Historical price trends
- Buyer demand
- Farmer location
- Buyer location
- Transportation cost
- Storage cost
- Produce quality
- Quantity
- Delivery requirements
- Buyer reliability
- Predicted price movement

It then ranks available selling opportunities.
